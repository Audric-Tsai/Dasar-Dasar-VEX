# 04 — Hubs & Adapters

Connector parts. They do exactly one job: let two parts with different bore
shapes join together.

Sounds trivial until you buy a 4" flex wheel and realise it won't mount to
anything you own.

---

## VersaHex Adapters

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-5949` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/4" Long) | `217-5950` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7946` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7947` |
| Plastic 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-8004-001` |

> `217-7946` and `217-7947` are listed with identical descriptions in the sheet.
> Two different part numbers can't be the same item. Following the `217-5949` /
> `217-5950` pattern, one of them is probably `1/4" Long`. **Check the product
> page before ordering.**

**What it does.** Converts a `1/4"` square bore — the standard VEX shaft size —
into a `1/2"` hex.

**Why it exists.** VEX has two different shaft systems. The main catalogue
(`276-`) uses square shafts. The VersaFrame line (`217-`) uses hex shafts. This
adapter bridges the two.

**About the lengths.** `1/8" Long` and `1/4" Long` refer to the thickness of the
adapter. Pick one to match the thickness of the part you're mounting. If the
adapter is thinner than the bore, the joint will wobble.

---

## VersaHub

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| 1.125" Bearing Bore Plastic VersaHub Spacer (1/2" Wide) | `217-2591` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Aluminum VersaHub | `217-2592` |
| 1.125" Bearing Pilot Plastic VersaHub (1/4" Wide, w/ Plate Sprocket Mount) | `217-3234` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub | `217-4009` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub v2 | `217-8079` |

**What it does.** Mounts into the centre of 3" and 4" flex wheels, giving those
wheels a `1/2"` hex bore so a shaft can drive them.

**Why it's mandatory.** The 3" and 4" flex wheels have a `1-1/8" ID` bore — round
and large. A round bore can't transmit rotation. Without a hub, the shaft spins
inside the wheel and the wheel just sits there.

**Plastic vs aluminium.** `217-2592` is aluminium — stronger, heavier, more
expensive. The rest are plastic. For intakes, plastic is fine.

**The v2 versions.** `217-8079` is the revised version of `217-4009`. Don't mix
two versions on one wheel — the tolerances can differ slightly.

---

## Spacer

`217-2591` is called a VersaHub **Spacer**, not a regular VersaHub. It isn't a
connector, it's a spacing part — it mounts next to a hub to get the wheel
positioned correctly.

---

## How to Choose

Start from the bore of your wheel, not from the part name.

| Wheel bore | What you need |
|---|---|
| `1/2" Hex` | Nothing. Straight onto a hex shaft. |
| `1/4" Square` | Nothing, if you're using standard VEX square shaft. |
| `1-1/8" ID` | You need a **VersaHub**. |
| Square shaft but a hex part | You need a **VersaHex Adapter**. |

---

## Common Mistakes

**Thinking hubs are optional.** The 3" and 4" flex wheels don't work without one.

**Forgetting the spacer.** The wheel mounts but slides around on the shaft.

**Mixing v1 and v2 in one mechanism.** If something binds and you can't work out
why, check that all your hubs are the same version.

# 04 — Hub & Adapter

Part penghubung. Fungsinya cuma satu: bikin dua part yang bentuk lubangnya beda
jadi bisa nyambung.

Kedengarannya sepele sampai kamu beli roda flex 4" dan sadar dia nggak bisa
dipasang ke apa pun yang kamu punya.

---

## VersaHex Adapter

| Part | Kode |
|---|---|
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-5949` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/4" Long) | `217-5950` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7946` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7947` |
| Plastic 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-8004-001` |

> `217-7946` dan `217-7947` ketulis dengan deskripsi persis sama di sheet. Dua
> nomor part beda nggak mungkin barangnya identik. Ngikutin pola `217-5949` /
> `217-5950`, kemungkinan besar yang satu `1/4" Long`. **Cek halaman produknya
> sebelum beli.**

**Fungsinya.** Ngubah lubang persegi `1/4"` — ukuran shaft VEX standar — jadi
hex `1/2"`.

**Kenapa ada.** VEX punya dua sistem shaft yang beda. Katalog utama (`276-`)
pakai shaft persegi. Lini VersaFrame (`217-`) pakai shaft hex. Adapter ini
jembatan antara keduanya.

**Soal panjang.** `1/8" Long` dan `1/4" Long` itu ketebalan adapternya. Pilih
sesuai setebal apa part yang mau kamu pasang. Kalau adapternya lebih tipis dari
lubangnya, sambungannya goyang.

---

## VersaHub

| Part | Kode |
|---|---|
| 1.125" Bearing Bore Plastic VersaHub Spacer (1/2" Wide) | `217-2591` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Aluminum VersaHub | `217-2592` |
| 1.125" Bearing Pilot Plastic VersaHub (1/4" Wide, w/ Plate Sprocket Mount) | `217-3234` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub | `217-4009` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub v2 | `217-8079` |

**Fungsinya.** Dipasang ke tengah roda flex 3" dan 4", ngasih roda itu lubang
hex `1/2"` biar bisa digerakin shaft.

**Kenapa wajib.** Roda flex 3" dan 4" lubangnya `1-1/8" ID` — bulat dan besar.
Lubang bulat nggak bisa nyalurin putaran. Tanpa hub, shaft muter di dalam roda
dan rodanya diem aja.

**Plastik vs aluminium.** `217-2592` aluminium — lebih kuat, lebih berat, lebih
mahal. Sisanya plastik. Buat intake, plastik cukup.

**Yang v2.** `217-8079` versi revisi dari `217-4009`. Jangan campur satu roda
pakai dua versi — toleransinya bisa beda tipis.

---

## Spacer

`217-2591` namanya VersaHub **Spacer**, bukan VersaHub biasa. Ini bukan
penghubung, ini pengatur jarak — dipasang di sebelah hub biar posisi rodanya pas
di tengah.

---

## Cara Milih

Mulai dari lubang roda kamu, bukan dari nama part-nya.

| Lubang roda | Yang kamu butuh |
|---|---|
| `1/2" Hex` | Nggak butuh apa-apa. Langsung ke shaft hex. |
| `1/4" Square` | Nggak butuh apa-apa kalau shaft kamu persegi VEX standar. |
| `1-1/8" ID` | Butuh **VersaHub**. |
| Shaft persegi tapi part-nya hex | Butuh **VersaHex Adapter**. |

---

## Yang Sering Salah

**Ngira hub itu opsional.** Roda flex 3" dan 4" nggak jalan tanpa hub.

**Lupa spacer.** Roda kepasang tapi geser-geser di shaft.

**Campur v1 dan v2 di satu mekanisme.** Kalau ada yang seret dan kamu nggak
ngerti kenapa, cek dulu semua hub-nya versi yang sama.

# 04 — 轮毂与转接件

连接类零件。它只干一件事：让两个孔形不同的零件能装到一起。

听起来没什么，直到你买了一个 4" 柔性轮，才发现它装不到你手上任何东西上。

---

## VersaHex 转接件

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-5949` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters (1/4" Square Bore, 1/4" Long) | `217-5950` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7946` |
| 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-7947` |
| Plastic 1/2" VersaHex Adapters v2 (1/4" Square Bore, 1/8" Long) | `217-8004-001` |

> `217-7946` 和 `217-7947` 在表格里的描述完全一样。两个不同的编号不可能是同一个东西。
> 按照 `217-5949` / `217-5950` 的规律，其中一个很可能是 `1/4" Long`。
> **下单前请先查产品页面。**

**它的作用。** 把 `1/4"` 方孔 —— VEX 标准轴尺寸 —— 转换成 `1/2"` 六角。

**为什么会有这个零件。** VEX 有两套不同的轴系统。主目录（`276-`）用方轴，
VersaFrame 系列（`217-`）用六角轴。这个转接件就是两者之间的桥梁。

**关于长度。** `1/8" Long` 和 `1/4" Long` 指的是转接件的厚度。
要按你要装的零件的厚度来选。如果转接件比孔薄，接合处会晃。

---

## VersaHub 轮毂

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| 1.125" Bearing Bore Plastic VersaHub Spacer (1/2" Wide) | `217-2591` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Aluminum VersaHub | `217-2592` |
| 1.125" Bearing Pilot Plastic VersaHub (1/4" Wide, w/ Plate Sprocket Mount) | `217-3234` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub | `217-4009` |
| 1/2" Hex Bore Plastic VersaHub v2 | `217-8079` |

**它的作用。** 装在 3" 和 4" 柔性轮的中心，给这些轮子提供一个 `1/2"` 六角孔，
这样轴才能带动它们。

**为什么是必须的。** 3" 和 4" 柔性轮的孔是 `1-1/8" ID` —— 又圆又大。
圆孔没法传递转动。没有轮毂，轴在轮子里空转，轮子纹丝不动。

**塑料还是铝。** `217-2592` 是铝的 —— 更结实、更重、更贵。其余都是塑料的。
做吸入机构，塑料就够了。

**v2 版本。** `217-8079` 是 `217-4009` 的改版。同一个轮子上不要混用两个版本 ——
公差可能有细微差别。

---

## 垫圈式轮毂（Spacer）

`217-2591` 叫 VersaHub **Spacer**，不是普通的 VersaHub。
它不是连接件，是调间距用的 —— 装在轮毂旁边，把轮子的位置摆正。

---

## 怎么选

从你轮子的孔开始判断，不要从零件名字开始。

| 轮子的孔 | 你需要什么 |
|---|---|
| `1/2" Hex` | 什么都不需要，直接装六角轴。 |
| `1/4" Square` | 如果你用的是 VEX 标准方轴，什么都不需要。 |
| `1-1/8" ID` | 需要 **VersaHub**。 |
| 方轴但零件是六角的 | 需要 **VersaHex Adapter**。 |

---

## 常见错误

**以为轮毂是可选的。** 3" 和 4" 柔性轮没有轮毂根本转不起来。

**忘了装 spacer。** 轮子装上了，但在轴上来回窜。

**同一个机构里混用 v1 和 v2。** 如果有什么地方发卡而你又想不通为什么，
先检查所有轮毂是不是同一个版本。
