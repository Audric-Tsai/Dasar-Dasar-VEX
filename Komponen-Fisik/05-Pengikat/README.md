# 05 — Fasteners

What holds everything together.

This section looks like the most boring one and gets underestimated most often.
But a robot that falls apart mid-match is almost always let down by its
fasteners, not its frame.

**The first thing to know: there are two completely different head systems.** Hex
drive and Star drive. Different heads, different drivers. Wrong driver and you
strip the head, and then the screw can't come out at all.

---

**Why VEX made two systems.** Star drive heads are much harder to strip. The bit
seats deeper, so more of the force you apply goes into the thread instead of into
wrecking the head.

**Star drive also comes in longer sizes** — up to 2.5", while hex stops at 1.25".
If you need a long screw, star drive is the only option.

**Reading the sizes.** `#8-32 x 1/2"`. `#8` = screw diameter. `32` = threads per
inch. `1/2"` = length. Usually only the length changes; `#8-32` is the VEX
standard.

**Locking screws** have a coating that resists loosening. **Set screws** are short
and headless, for locking collars. **Shoulder screws** have a smooth unthreaded
section so a part can rotate on it.

## Nuts & Washers

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| #8-32 Keps Nut | `275-1026` |
| #8-32 Nylock Nut | `275-1027` |
| #8-32 Hex Nut | `275-1028` |
| #8-32 Low Profile Nut | `276-7767` |
| Steel Washer | `275-1024` |
| Teflon Washer | `275-1025` |

**Nylock** has a nylon ring inside. Doesn't work loose from vibration.
**Mandatory on drivetrains and anything that moves.**

**Keps** has a toothed washer built into the base. Faster to fit, easier to work
loose. Prototypes only.

**Hex nut** is plain. Needs some other locking method.

**Low profile nut** is thinner. For tight spaces.

**Teflon washers** are slippery — used where a part rotates, to cut friction.
**Steel washers** spread the load of the screw head.

**When in doubt, use nylock.** Stripping the robot down because a screw fell out
mid-match costs far more than the extra time fitting nylocks.

**What they do.** Help hold an arm up so the motor doesn't have to work as hard.
Common on mechanisms that need to return to a starting position.

**#32 vs #64** are standard rubber band sizes. `#64` is longer and thicker.

**Three materials.** Silicone is stretchiest, EPDM lasts longest, synthetic is
cheapest. EPDM is the newest and usually the best for competition.

**Latex tubing** is stronger still. For large spring forces, like launching
mechanisms.

---

## Common Mistakes

**Wrong driver.** An Allen key in a star drive head strips it permanently. Check
the head first.

**Screws too long.** The end catches on a gear or shaft next to it. The robot
jams and you can't work out why.

**Keps nuts on a drivetrain.** They work loose mid-match and a wheel comes off.

**Mixing spacer ODs.** A 3/8" won't retain a part that needs 1/2".

# 05 — Pengikat

Yang nyatuin semuanya.

Bagian ini kelihatan paling membosankan dan paling sering diremehin. Padahal
robot yang lepas di tengah match hampir selalu gara-gara pengikatnya, bukan
rangkanya.

**Yang harus kamu tau duluan: ada dua sistem kepala baut yang beda total.** Hex
drive dan Star drive. Beda kepala, beda obeng. Salah obeng, kepala bautnya
rusak dan bautnya nggak bisa dilepas lagi.

## Yang Sering Salah

**Salah obeng.** Kunci L di kepala star drive bikin kepala slek permanen. Cek
kepalanya dulu.

**Baut kepanjangan.** Ujungnya nyangkut ke gear atau shaft di sebelahnya. Robot
macet dan kamu bingung kenapa.

**Pakai keps nut di drivetrain.** Kendor di tengah match, roda copot.

**Campur spacer beda OD.** Yang 3/8" nggak nahan part yang butuh 1/2".

# 05 — 紧固件

把所有东西固定在一起的零件。

这一章看起来最枯燥，也最常被轻视。但比赛中途散架的机器人，
问题几乎总是出在紧固件上，而不是骨架。

**首先要知道：有两套完全不同的螺丝头系统。** Hex drive（内六角）和
Star drive（梅花）。头不一样，工具也不一样。工具用错，
螺丝头就被拧花了，之后连拆都拆不下来。

**VEX 为什么要做两套系统。** 梅花头更不容易拧花。批头咬得更深，
你施加的力更多进到螺纹里，而不是用来把螺丝头拧坏。

**梅花螺丝还有更长的规格** —— 最长到 2.5"，而内六角只到 1.25"。
需要长螺丝的话，梅花是唯一选择。

**怎么看尺寸。** `#8-32 x 1/2"`。`#8` = 螺丝直径。`32` = 每英寸牙数。
`1/2"` = 长度。通常只有长度会变；`#8-32` 是 VEX 的标准规格。

**Locking screw（防松螺丝）** 表面有涂层，不容易松。
**Set screw（顶丝）** 短而无头，用来锁紧轴环。
**Shoulder screw（台肩螺丝）** 有一段光滑无螺纹的部分，零件可以在上面转动。

## 螺母和垫圈

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| #8-32 Keps Nut | `275-1026` |
| #8-32 Nylock Nut | `275-1027` |
| #8-32 Hex Nut | `275-1028` |
| #8-32 Low Profile Nut | `276-7767` |
| Steel Washer | `275-1024` |
| Teflon Washer | `275-1025` |

**Nylock（尼龙防松螺母）** 内部有一圈尼龙，不会被震松。
**底盘和所有运动部件上必须用这个。**

**Keps** 底部自带一圈带齿垫片。装起来快，但更容易松。只适合做原型。

**Hex nut（普通六角螺母）** 没有任何防松设计，需要另配锁紧手段。

**Low profile nut（薄螺母）** 更薄，用在空间紧张的地方。

**Teflon washer（特氟龙垫圈）** 很滑 —— 用在有零件转动的地方，减少摩擦。
**Steel washer（钢垫圈）** 用来分散螺丝头的压力。

**拿不准就用 nylock。** 比赛中途螺丝掉了要整台拆开重装，
代价远高于装 nylock 多花的那点时间。

**它的作用。** 帮忙托住机械臂，让电机不用那么吃力。

**#32 和 #64** 是橡皮筋的标准尺寸。`#64` 更长也更粗。

**三种材料。** 硅胶最有弹性，EPDM 最耐用，合成橡胶最便宜。
EPDM 是最新的，通常也最适合比赛。

**Latex tubing（乳胶管）** 比橡皮筋更强。用于需要很大弹力的场合，比如发射机构。

## 常见错误

**用错工具。** 拿内六角扳手去拧梅花头，会把头永久拧花。先看清楚头型。

**螺丝太长。** 露出的一端刮到旁边的齿轮或轴。机器人卡住，你还想不通为什么。

**底盘上用 keps 螺母。** 比赛中途松掉，轮子就掉了。

**混用不同外径的隔离柱。** 3/8" 的挡不住需要 1/2" 的零件。
