# 10 — Miscellaneous

Parts that don't fit anywhere else. They look trivial, but a few of them decide
whether your robot finishes the match.

---

## Anti-Slip Mat

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| Thick Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0120` |
| Thin Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0121` |

Rubber sheet stuck onto any surface that needs to grip a game object.

**This is one of the highest-impact parts per unit of money.** A mechanism that
kept slipping can start working the moment you stick mat on it. Thick for maximum
grip, thin for tight spaces.

---

## Zip Ties & Adhesives

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| 4" Zip Ties | `276-1032` |
| 11" Zip Ties | `275-0125` |
| Adhesive Foam | `275-1254` |
| Hook & Loop Adhesive Strip (5') | `275-1257` |
| Hook & Loop Strap (5') | `275-1259` |
| Cinch Strap | `275-1258` |

**Zip ties** for tidying cables. **Don't dismiss these.** Loose cable can catch in
a wheel or get yanked out, and a motor with a disconnected cable is a dead motor.

**Hook & loop** (velcro) for holding a battery or any part you open often.

**Cinch straps** for ties you can undo and redo repeatedly without cutting.

**Adhesive foam** for absorbing impact.

---

## PET Sheet

| Part | Code |
|---|---|
| 2x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-001` |
| 6x6 PET Sheets | `276-8341-002` |
| 6x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-003` |

Clear plastic sheet you can cut and bend to whatever shape you need. For guides,
covers, or retaining walls inside the robot.

**The area of plastic you're allowed to use is limited every season.** Check the
Game Manual before you cut.

---

## Common Mistakes

**Using no zip ties at all.** A cable wraps around a wheel mid-match.

**Forgetting anti-slip mat.** You add motors and gears to solve a problem that
actually just needed a small piece of rubber.

**Cutting PET without checking the rules.** The area is capped, and the robot can
be disqualified.

# 10 — Lain-Lain

Part yang nggak masuk kategori lain. Kelihatan sepele, tapi beberapa di antaranya
nentuin robot kamu selesai match atau nggak.

---

## Anti-Slip Mat

| Part | Kode |
|---|---|
| Thick Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0120` |
| Thin Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0121` |

Lembaran karet yang ditempel di permukaan yang harus nyengkeram objek permainan.

**Ini salah satu part paling berpengaruh per rupiah.** Mekanisme yang tadinya
selip terus bisa langsung jalan cuma karena ditempelin mat. Tebal buat
cengkeraman maksimal, tipis buat tempat sempit.

---

## Zip Tie & Perekat

| Part | Kode |
|---|---|
| 4" Zip Ties | `276-1032` |
| 11" Zip Ties | `275-0125` |
| Adhesive Foam | `275-1254` |
| Hook & Loop Adhesive Strip (5') | `275-1257` |
| Hook & Loop Strap (5') | `275-1259` |
| Cinch Strap | `275-1258` |

**Zip tie** buat ngerapiin kabel. **Jangan sepelein ini.** Kabel berantakan bisa
nyangkut di roda atau ketarik lepas, dan motor yang kabelnya lepas itu motor
mati.

**Hook & loop** (velcro) buat nahan baterai atau part yang harus sering dibuka.

**Cinch strap** buat ngiket yang bisa dilepas berulang kali tanpa dipotong.

**Adhesive foam** buat ngeredam benturan.

---

## Lembaran PET

| Part | Kode |
|---|---|
| 2x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-001` |
| 6x6 PET Sheets | `276-8341-002` |
| 6x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-003` |

Lembaran plastik bening yang bisa dipotong dan ditekuk sesuai kebutuhan. Buat
pengarah objek, penutup, atau dinding penahan di dalam robot.

**Luas plastik yang boleh dipakai dibatasi tiap musim.** Cek Game Manual sebelum
motong.

---

## Yang Sering Salah

**Nggak pakai zip tie sama sekali.** Kabel kelilit roda di tengah match.

**Lupa anti-slip mat.** Kamu nambah motor dan gear buat masalah yang sebenarnya
cuma butuh karet seharga sedikit.

**Motong PET tanpa cek aturan.** Luasnya dibatasi, dan robot bisa didiskualifikasi.

# 10 — 其他

放不进其他分类的零件。看起来不起眼，但其中几样决定了
你的机器人能不能撑到比赛结束。

---

## 防滑垫（Anti-Slip Mat）

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| Thick Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0120` |
| Thin Anti-Slip Mat | `275-0121` |

贴在任何需要抓住场地道具的表面上的橡胶片。

**论性价比，这是影响最大的零件之一。** 一直打滑的机构，
可能贴上防滑垫的那一刻就好用了。厚的抓力最强，薄的适合空间紧张处。

---

## 扎带和粘接件

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| 4" Zip Ties | `276-1032` |
| 11" Zip Ties | `275-0125` |
| Adhesive Foam | `275-1254` |
| Hook & Loop Adhesive Strip (5') | `275-1257` |
| Hook & Loop Strap (5') | `275-1259` |
| Cinch Strap | `275-1258` |

**扎带** 用来整理线缆。**别小看它。** 松散的线可能缠进轮子里，
或者被拽脱，而线掉了的电机就是废的电机。

**Hook & loop（魔术贴）** 用来固定电池，或者任何需要经常打开的部件。

**Cinch strap（可调绑带）** 可以反复解开重绑，不用剪断。

**Adhesive foam（背胶泡棉）** 用来缓冲撞击。

---

## PET 板

| 零件 | 编号 |
|---|---|
| 2x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-001` |
| 6x6 PET Sheets | `276-8341-002` |
| 6x12 PET Sheets | `276-8341-003` |

透明塑料板，可以按需要裁剪和弯折。用作导向板、盖板，
或者机器人内部的挡墙。

**允许使用的塑料面积每个赛季都有限制。** 动剪刀之前先查 Game Manual。

---

## 常见错误

**完全不用扎带。** 比赛中途线缆缠进轮子。

**忘了用防滑垫。** 你为了一个其实只需要一小片橡胶的问题，
加了一堆电机和齿轮。

**没查规则就裁 PET。** 面积是有上限的，机器人可能被判违规。
