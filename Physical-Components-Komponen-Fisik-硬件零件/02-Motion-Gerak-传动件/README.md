VEX shafts are named in **pitch**, not inches directly.

**1x pitch = 0.5 inches.** So `4x Pitch` = 2 inches, `12x Pitch` = 6 inches.

### Bearing Flat — the part everyone forgets

A small white plastic ring that mounts in a C-channel hole where a shaft passes
through.

Remember those square C-channel holes? If you push a square shaft straight through
a square hole, it can't spin smoothly — corner meets corner, and it binds and
drags. A bearing flat turns the square hole into a round one so the shaft spins
freely.

A robot without bearings: wobbly wheels, hot motors, battery draining fast.

**The rule is simple: every shaft that passes through a C-channel needs a bearing.**

### Shaft Collars

Metal rings tightened onto a shaft so it can't slide sideways.

**Clamping collars** grip the shaft from the outside. Stronger, and they don't
damage the shaft. The ordinary ones use a set screw, which can dent it.

### Pillow Blocks

A bearing that mounts on a surface rather than inside a hole. Use it to support a
shaft somewhere there's no C-channel.

**The basic rule.** Small gear driving a big gear → slower, stronger. Big gear
driving a small gear → faster, weaker. Nothing is free.

**Example.** Motor has a 12T gear, wheel has a 60T gear. Ratio is 60:12 = 5:1. The
wheel turns 5 times slower than the motor, but with 5 times the torque.

**Worm gears** give a very large reduction in a small space, and **they can't be
back-driven from the output side**. That means an arm can hold its position without
the motor running constantly.

**Bevel gears** turn the direction of rotation by 90°.

**Rack gears** convert rotation into straight-line motion.

**The v2 line** (24T through 84T High Strength) replaces v1. If you're buying new,
get v2.

## Common Mistakes

**Forgetting bearings.** Number one. Shaft straight through the C-channel, robot
drags, motors overheat.

**Forgetting shaft collars.** The shaft creeps sideways until the gears no longer
mesh.

**Thinking "4x Pitch" means 4 inches.** It's 2 inches. Halve it.

**Mixing HS and regular systems.** HS bearings, collars, and gears don't fit
regular shafts.

**Mixing sprocket systems.** The chain won't connect.

### Bearing Flat — part paling sering dilupain

Ring plastik putih yang dipasang di lubang C-channel, tempat shaft lewat.

Ingat lubang C-channel yang kotak? Kalau shaft persegi kamu masukin langsung ke

Yang biasa pakai set screw yang bisa bikin penyok.

### Pillow Block

Bearing yang dipasang di permukaan, bukan di dalam lubang. Buat nopang shaft di
tempat yang nggak ada C-channel-nya.

### Bearing Flat —— 最容易被忘掉的零件

装在 C 型槽孔位上的白色塑料小环，传动轴从中间穿过。

还记得 C 型槽的方孔吗？如果你把方轴直接插进方孔里，它没法顺畅转动 ——
角对着角，会卡、会拖。Bearing flat 把方孔变成圆孔，轴就能自由转动了。

没装轴承的机器人：轮子晃、电机发烫、电池掉得飞快。

**规则很简单：每一根穿过 C 型槽的轴，都必须装轴承。**

### 轴环（Shaft Collar）

锁在轴上的金属环，防止轴左右窜动。

**Clamping collar（夹紧式轴环）** 从外面抱住轴，更牢固，也不会损伤轴。
普通款用顶丝，会把轴顶出凹坑。

### Pillow Block（轴承座）

装在表面上而不是装在孔里的轴承。用来在没有 C 型槽的地方支撑传动轴。

**基本规则。** 小齿轮带大齿轮 → 更慢、更有力。大齿轮带小齿轮 → 更快、更没劲。
天下没有免费的午餐。

**举例。** 电机上装 12T 齿轮，轮子上装 60T 齿轮。传动比 60:12 = 5:1。
轮子转速是电机的五分之一，但扭矩是五倍。

**蜗轮蜗杆（Worm gear）** 能在很小的空间里做出极大的减速比，而且
**不能从输出端反向驱动**。这意味着机械臂可以保持位置，不用一直通电。

**伞齿轮（Bevel gear）** 把转动方向拐 90°。

**齿条（Rack gear）** 把转动变成直线运动。

**v2 系列**（24T 到 84T High Strength）取代了 v1。新买的话，买 v2。

## 常见错误

**忘装轴承。** 排第一。轴直接穿过 C 型槽，机器人拖沓，电机过热。

**忘装轴环。** 轴慢慢往外窜，直到齿轮啮合不上。

**以为 "4x Pitch" 是 4 英寸。** 是 2 英寸。除以二。

**混用 HS 和普通系统。** HS 的轴承、轴环、齿轮装不上普通轴。

**混用不同系统的链轮。** 链条接不上。
