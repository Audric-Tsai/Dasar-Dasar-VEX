ODOMETRY
========================================================================

English · Bahasa Indonesia · 简体中文

------------------------------------------------------------------------
EN — English
------------------------------------------------------------------------

What is odometry?

Odometry is how a robot keeps track of its own position and heading
(its "pose": x, y, and angle) on the field by measuring how much its
wheels have turned, instead of relying on an outside reference like a
camera or a beacon. The robot starts from a known point and continuously
updates its estimated location as it moves — this approach is often
called "dead reckoning."

What is it used for in VEX?

In VEX, odometry is usually built from small unpowered tracking wheels
fitted with encoders (sometimes combined with an inertial sensor for
heading), mounted on the drivetrain. As the robot drives, the encoders
measure how far the tracking wheels roll, and that data — combined with
the wheel size and the geometry of where the tracking wheels are
mounted — is turned into an estimate of the robot's exact position and
orientation on the field.

This matters because it lets a team write autonomous routines that
target actual field coordinates ("drive to x=24, y=48") instead of
simple "drive forward for this many inches" commands. It also lets the
robot detect and correct drift if a wheel slips or the robot gets
bumped, which is why most competitive autonomous and skills routines
rely on it.

Apa itu odometry?

Odometry adalah cara robot buat ngelacak posisi dan arah hadapnya
sendiri (disebut "pose": x, y, dan sudut) di lapangan, dengan cara
ngukur seberapa banyak rodanya udah muter — bukan pakai referensi luar
kayak kamera atau beacon. Robot mulai dari titik yang udah diketahui,
terus posisinya terus di-update tiap dia gerak. Cara ini sering disebut
"dead reckoning."

Buat apa dipakai di VEX?

Di VEX, odometry biasanya dibikin pakai tracking wheel — roda kecil
tanpa motor yang dipasangin encoder (kadang digabung sama inertial
sensor buat ngukur arah hadap) — yang dipasang di drivetrain. Pas robot
jalan, encoder ngukur seberapa jauh tracking wheel-nya muter, terus data
itu — digabung sama ukuran roda dan posisi pemasangan tracking wheel-nya
— diolah jadi perkiraan posisi dan arah hadap robot yang akurat di
lapangan.

Ini penting karena bikin tim bisa nulis rute autonomous yang nargetin
koordinat lapangan beneran ("jalan ke x=24, y=48"), bukan cuma perintah
"jalan maju sekian inci." Odometry juga bikin robot bisa nyadar dan
ngoreksi diri kalau ada roda yang selip atau kesenggol, makanya
kebanyakan rute autonomous dan skills kompetitif pakai ini.

什么是里程计（Odometry）？

里程计是机器人通过测量轮子转动了多少，来持续跟踪自己在场地上的位
置和朝向（也就是它的“位姿”：x、y 坐标和角度）的方法，而不依赖摄
像头或信标之类的外部参照物。机器人从一个已知的起始点出发，随着它
的移动不断更新自己估计的位置——这种方法通常叫做“航位推算”
（dead reckoning）。

在 VEX 中它有什么用？

在 VEX 里，里程计通常是靠安装在底盘上的小型从动轮（tracking
wheel）实现的，这些轮子装有编码器（有时还会搭配惯性传感器来测量朝
向）。机器人移动时，编码器会测量这些从动轮转动的距离，再结合轮子
的尺寸和从动轮的安装位置，就能计算出机器人在场地上的精确位置和朝
向。

这很重要，因为它能让团队编写以实际场地坐标为目标的自动阶段程序
（比如“开到 x=24, y=48”），而不只是简单的“前进多少英寸”这种指
令。它还能让机器人在轮子打滑或被碰撞时察觉并修正偏差，这也是大多
数比赛级自动阶段和 skills 程序都依赖它的原因。
